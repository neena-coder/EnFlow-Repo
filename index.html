The problem

Every service, the same scramble.

The choir picks the songs. Someone has to get every verse onto the screen at the right moment, while the song is already moving.

The usual way

  • Copy each verse from a document, one by one.
  • Paste or retype it into the display software during the service.
  • Fix typos while the choir is already singing.
  • Lose your place the moment the song repeats a chorus.

With EnFlow

  • Import the song as a text file before the service.
  • Lyrics are split into pages for you, 4 lines at a time or whatever you set.
  • Preview the next page, then send it live with Next.
  • Move around the song with shortcuts, not the mouse.

Questions

Before you subscribe.

What kind of file do I load?

A plain text file. Put one lyric line per line. EnFlow numbers the lines and groups them into pages for you.

Do I need OBS?

Yes. EnFlow is the operator panel and OBS is what puts the lyrics on your screen or stream. The footer shows Output Connected once they are talking.

How many lines show at once?

You choose. The Lines Per Page box sets it, and the page changes as you press Next and Previous.

Which computers does it run on?

EnFlow 1.0.0.1 is built for Windows.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next month starts. Yearly plans run for the year you paid for.
